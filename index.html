Living manifesto

This is a working document: updated in public as we listen, measure results, and adjust. It's short on slogans and long on delivery.

How we'll govern: publish plans, publish budgets, publish progress. The council controls just 5% of Male' revenue — we'll make every laari count.

How you'll participate: neighborhood visits, open office hours, and a public issue tracker you can follow at our transparency ledger.

How we'll be judged: clear targets for cleanliness, safety, response times, and affordability — serving 41% of the nation's population on this 2 km² island.
